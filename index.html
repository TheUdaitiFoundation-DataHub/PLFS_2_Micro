Click a column header to sort. All-India figures are pinned at the top for reference.
Overall Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR) and Overall Worker Population Ratio (WPR) are for persons aged 15 years and above, usual status (ps+ss), rural + urban combined. Employed – Agriculture and Employed – Manufacturing correspond to NIC-2008 industry sections A and C respectively; Employed – Services aggregates the remaining sections (B and D through U) as a percentage of usually working persons. Overall Workforce is the scaled total number of employed persons by State/UT. Sector employment columns may not add up to 100% for a given state, as sectors such as Construction and Mining & Quarrying are reported separately and not included in the Agriculture, Manufacturing or Services figures shown here.
Source: Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2025.
